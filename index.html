The Abiha Libas Atelier

“True luxury lies in the patient rhythm of the handloom and the quiet shimmer of pure zari.”

Working directly with third-generation weaver clusters in Varanasi, Chanderi, and Lucknow, ABIHA LIBAS preserves dying couture techniques—translating archival royal motifs into weightless modern heirlooms.

Heritage & Philosophy

Woven with Reverence, Tailored for Today

Founded with a devotion to slow, intentional Indian couture, ABIHA LIBAS bridges the grandeur of royal darbars with the ease of contemporary dressing. Every garment undergoes an 18-stage quality and hand-finishing ritual before leaving our atelier.

Whether you are selecting a bridal heirloom lehenga or an effortless mulmul kurta for intimate gatherings, our pieces are designed to be cherished and passed down across generations.
